🦝 About FERAL AI
🧠 Local brain for apps, devices and memory — FERAL runs locally as the control plane between your software and hardware. It keeps long‑lived memory, learns your baseline patterns and executes digital‑twin actions with explicit policy gates.
⚙️ Connects to every device — wearables, smart home gear, smart glasses, robots, your computer, your phone and more.
🗣️ Sub‑200 ms voice — wake‑word detection and streaming via OpenAI and Gemini: talk to your devices hands‑free.
⚡ Proactive intelligence — watches your baseline across health, screen, calendar and environment; surfaces morning briefings, anomaly alerts and coaching.
🎨 Generative UI — developers ship headless API/CLI contracts and FERAL renders structured Gen‑UI surfaces locally.
🕹️ Adjustable autonomy — strict, hybrid or loose. You decide how much control to hand over.
👥 Digital Twin — builds a model of your preferences, decisions and reasoning from your history.
🥽 About THEORA Smart Glasses
❤️ Heart rate & SpO₂ tracking — smart glasses in development that track your heart rate, SpO₂, UV exposure and body temperature.
☀️ UV monitor & temperature — sensors monitor UV exposure and your body temperature through contact with your skin.
🎙️ Voice assistant & translation — built‑in voice assistant and 62‑language translation.
👓 Integrated sensors — sensors in the nose pads and temples measure vital signs via skin contact.
🕶️ In development — join the waitlist to be among the first to experience THEORA.
